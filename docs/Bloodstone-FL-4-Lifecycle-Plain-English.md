# FL-4 “Lifecycle only” — plain English

**Yes — platform salvage is fully removed.** FL-4 is **not** salvage coming back under another name.

---

## Two different things

| | **Salvage (REMOVED from platform)** | **Lifecycle (KEPT — FL-4)** |
|--|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **What it is** | Paying or scoring people for “killing” coins (leaderboard, badges, LRGK redeem, graveyard board) | A **status field** on each fork: is it live, quiet, or dead? |
| **Why it existed** | Culture + (old) payout | So the catalog doesn’t lie (“this coin is live” when the seed is dead) |
| **Platform?** | **No — gone** from Fork Lab / Bloodstone | **Yes — boring registry hygiene** |
| **Money?** | Was payout → **removed** | **No money** |
| **Goblin Magic** | LRGK-only canary, off platform | Not required for FL-4 |

---

## What lifecycle actually does

Think **shipping status**, not a game:

1. **Live** — seed tip OK, listed as mineable  
2. **Declining / ember** — quiet or building  
3. **Inactive / ash** — rugged, retired, offline  

Fork Lab may **probe RPC** and update a row in the registry so explorers and the store stay honest.

It does **not**:

- pay STONE / LRGK / anything  
- give badges or leaderboards  
- open a public “graveyard salvage” board on Bloodstone  

That’s why the RFC title is **“Lifecycle hygiene only (no platform salvage).”**
