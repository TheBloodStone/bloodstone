# FL-4 — Lifecycle Tracking (Registry Hygiene Only)

**Status:** Draft filed · provisional ID FL-4  
**Source of truth:** RFQ v1.5 §7, Decision #11, §12.4  
**Constitution:** Art. X — platform keeps hygiene only; salvage is **not** platform

## 1. Summary

Platform tracks fork **lifecycle state** (e.g. live / declining / inactive) for catalog honesty.  
**No platform salvage:** no payout, no leaderboard, no badges, no public graveyard board, no farm-lock.

## 2. Mechanism (from RFQ)

- Append-only / status fields for registry hygiene.  
- Optional RPC tip probing for “is the seed up?”  
- Optional vitality **display** metric — never a fee lever (Decision #12).  
- **Goblin Magic / salvage** runs only as **LRGK off-platform canary** if at all — not Fork Lab “what you get.”

## 3. Art. VII (incl. Q13)

1–3. No consensus measurement of “community health.”  
4–5. Display metrics must not claim security.  
6–7. No platform economic transfer for death of forks.  
13. **Neutrality:** Platform does not pay or privilege any coin for salvage. Coin-specific culture lives on that coin’s surface only (Art. X.2).

## 4. Build implication

Retire any platform Scrap Smelt redeem / salvage economy. LRGK may keep canary pages under LRGK branding only.
